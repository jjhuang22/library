## Corrections applied

The official page's labeled corrections state all four:

- H100s are not sold individually, even to hyperscalers; they are supplied eight at a time as part of DGX/HGX servers. The companion corrects the purchasing description and omits the spoken per-chip/bundle margin arithmetic.
- The majority of the write-down discussed came from data center, rather than primarily softer crypto demand. The reversal chapter expressly applies this correction.
- RNNs and CNNs both existed for a long time and served different tasks (language versus vision), rather than representing a simple state-of-the-art succession. The Transformer chapter expressly applies this correction.
- Attention predates the 2017 Transformer paper. The Transformer chapter distinguishes the paper's architecture from invention of attention.
