## Consequential qualifications

Family stories, Smarties agreements and confrontations remain uncertain accounts. Commodity-trading profits remain rumors. ROTA shorthand does not establish an exact accounting formula, and eighteen percent is not equated mathematically with four-year simple payback. The integration chapter preserves the hosts’ differing impulse-purchase estimates.

Wrigley financing is rounded: roughly $23 billion total, with $11 billion Mars cash, $5.7 billion bank debt and $6.5 billion Berkshire financing. Berkshire debt was repaid in 2013; preferred equity was bought out in 2016. The debt and equity therefore have different holding periods. Preferred dividends remain estimated.

Kellanova’s $35.9 billion agreement remains pending at the December 2024 recording. Pet care’s 59% revenue share and employee count do not establish division-level profit. The century-long fourteen-percent characterization concerns revenue, not shareholder returns. Powers, health expectations and the closing explanation remain the hosts’ arguments.
