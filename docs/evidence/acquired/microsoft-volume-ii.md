## Additional source qualifications

- **Online strategy:** original proprietary MSN differs from the later web property. Allard’s memo, Cornell and the offsite explain recognition without claiming inevitability. The scene illustrates this transition conceptually.
- **Browser development:** Mosaic and Netscape names and codebases differ. Spyglass licensing still required substantial IE engineering. Free distribution defended Windows. Sweeping market-share language is not copied as a literal 100% measurement. The Apple investment, Office commitment, IP settlement and IE default are all included. The inconsistent Google-founding reference at L176 is not copied.
- **Antitrust:** distinguish FTC deadlock, the 1994 decree, product versus feature, the 1997 order, the broader 1998 suit, deposition strategy, monopoly power, unlawful conduct, the breakup remedy, appeal and settlement. No blanket acquittal or criminal-double-jeopardy finding is claimed. The market decline overlapped the bubble and CEO change. Cultural costs, Ballmer’s support and Brad Smith’s peacemaking remain, with the hosts’ causal disagreement and the limits of knowing Gates’ internal state.
- **Enterprise and agreements:** clients, servers, applications and identity reinforce each other. The contract’s device measure is qualified rather than generalized to every employee. The cited 2007 estimates are 40% multi-year EA, 15% single-year EA, 30% OEM and 15% other. Low copy cost is not zero overall cost. IT and employee incentives differ; compatibility is a strength and a constraint.
- **XP and Xbox:** preserve NT unification and the consumer success. Entertainment and Devices reporting is not standalone Xbox profit. Financial returns, talent scarcity and Xbox Live capabilities are distinguished, with the hosts’ disagreement. No claim that XP SP2 could never have a virus.
- **Longhorn and Vista:** retain the three pillars, security delay, integration reset, poor reception, talent departure and shift toward the web. Avoid literalizing the mirror’s extreme compilation claim. Consumer relevance is the hosts’ judgment, with enterprise and legacy developer exceptions.
- **Search and deals:** preserve advertising economics, the $47 billion Yahoo proposal, assets and integration counterfactuals, the $240 million/1.6% Facebook investment and international ad rights. Unknown investment returns remain hypothetical. The resource argument distinguishes cash from talent.
- **Mobile and Windows 7/8:** distinguish the OEM ingredient model from the new consumer promise; Android’s open-source system from Google’s service package; illustrative license-fee arithmetic from actual contract rates. Retain Metro’s Zune lineage, Windows 7’s recovery, the iPad threat and timing. ARM and Surface RT are not all Windows 8 hardware.
- **Cloud:** preserve Ozzie/Mundie succession, the 2005 memo, protected incubation from 2006, Cutler/Srivastava, Windows Azure PaaS versus later infrastructure and open-source support, and disagreement about a company-wide bet. Ballmer’s support and Nadella’s execution both receive credit. Intelligent Cloud’s $88 billion FY2023 revenue includes legacy server products; it is not Azure alone. Hybrid relationships and earlier Bing, Xbox Live, Hotmail and MSN capabilities matter.
- **iPad, Nokia and leadership:** preserve the shelf party and the qualified incumbent rationale, including uncertainty about money lost by delaying. The August 23 retirement announcement and September 3 Nokia agreement do not establish the private bargaining process. The February 4, 2014 leadership change closes the main history.
- **Full concluding analysis:** retain $23–84 billion revenue, $12–30 billion operating income, 75x–14x earnings context, the qualified storytelling argument, incumbent peer comparison, changed Powers, flat-stock/stack-ranking incentives, embracing user behavior with viable economics, timing and bracketing, complementary leaders, partner constraints, and both final takeaways. Scale/switching costs remain strong; process and consumer brand weaken, enterprise trust strengthens, network and cornered-resource claims are weak, and hybrid counter-positioning is limited. These are the hosts’ interpretations, not new forecasts or independent causal findings.
