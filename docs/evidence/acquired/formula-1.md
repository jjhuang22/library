## Corrections, qualifications and timing

- Official correction: global transport/logistics is outsourced to **Rock-It**; the recording’s claim that Formula 1 itself supplies all logistics is not repeated as fact.
- Team-level strategy analysis applies principally to the **front half of the grid**. The hosts separately identify negative feedback loops at the back; the companion preserves that limitation.
- Prices, estimates, causal arguments and forecasts remain in the March 2026 recording’s context. Revenue, operating income, enterprise value, equity value and film box office are distinct. The $7/$127 comparison is the hosts’ rough sport-revenue/fan calculation, not a measured customer-cohort ARPU comparison.
- Circuit navigation uses only the Apple publisher chapter set: 00:00; 00:05:52; 00:30:43; 00:37:42; 00:50:33; 01:08:08; 01:33:18; 02:05:07; 02:42:33; 03:05:03; 03:26:45; 03:41:52; 03:56:23. Later analysis is combined editorially; no extra audio boundary is invented. Endpoint **04:20:08** is that set’s final listed cue (Carve-Outs + Outro), not an exact recording length. It is not combined with the mirror’s 04:28:10 farewell. Interlude passage references use mirror timing and are identified separately.
- The winding circuit preserves chapter-corner placement; elapsed time is interpolated between verified cues along its route, so route distance is nonuniform in time. Reading progress is not audio playback. Advertisements and player versions may shift listening positions.
