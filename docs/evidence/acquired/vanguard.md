## Official corrections applied

The episode page's labeled corrections override the original spoken errors:

- Joseph E. Welch was Wellington Management president/CEO July 1962–1972; Bogle became EVP in 1965 and chairman in 1970. No direct Morgan-to-Bogle CEO handover in 1965.
- ARVD events are electrical rhythm events, rather than the coronary-blockage events generally meant by “heart attacks.”
- Ivest was reorganized into Vanguard US Growth, retaining its 1959 inception date. Windsor launched in 1958; John Neff took over in 1964.
- Large producer-owned agricultural cooperatives, including Land O'Lakes, CHS, Ocean Spray, Organic Valley, and Tillamook, qualify the hosts' claim that cooperatives remain small. Producer ownership is distinguished from Vanguard's customer ownership.
